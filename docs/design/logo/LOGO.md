# Jugaad Street logo concepts

Files: `concept_N_mark.svg` (mark on transparent), `concept_N_icon.svg` (rounded square on tomato, subtle top-to-bottom gradient), `concept_N_lockup.svg` (light bg) and `concept_N_lockup_rev.svg` (on tomato). Wordmark is "jugaad street" in Plus Jakarta Sans 800, converted to outlines (no font dependency). Board: `board.png` (source `board.html`).

1. **Slice**: one circle cut into three shares by two parallel cuts (tomato / teal / peach). Bold, but reads a little like a pill or sliced fruit; weakest "marketplace" cue.
2. **Bag + Chat**: a shopping bag whose lower-left corner is a chat-bubble tail, split down the middle in teal and tomato. Says "buy together in chat" literally; busiest at 48 px.
3. **Basket of Three**: a half-disc bowl with a handle, the bowl cut into three segments (tomato / teal / peach) - three neighbours sharing one basket. Evolves the current basket mark; reads at 48 px.
4. **J Cup**: a "J" whose hook is a cup holding one round item split in two halves. Most ownable monogram and simplest; sharing cue is subtle.

## Recommendation: 3, Basket of Three
It keeps continuity with the existing basket idea, shows "one thing, split among a few people" instantly, uses all three brand colours with only three shapes plus a handle, and stays legible at 48 px (white segments on tomato). Runner-up: 4 (J Cup) if a pure letter-mark is preferred; it could serve as a favicon/avatar alongside the basket.

Notes: concept 2's tail and concept 1's slices could be simplified further before adoption. App assets are untouched.
