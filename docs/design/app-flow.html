Wholesale prices for your whole street

Jugaad Street is a bulk-buying marketplace for local communities. Neighbours bucket their orders inside their community chat to unlock wholesale prices from local wholesalers, farmers, fishermen and service providers. Members either hold money with UPI, paid only when the bucket fills, or simply book their share and pay when they meet at the market. A full bucket becomes an order with every member's details. Use the switcher at the top to see every screen in each design direction.

Four ways to bucket

SplitOne big item, different partsA whole seer fish, a Bakrid goat in 7 shares, a jackfruit. Each neighbour claims the part they want on a tappable picture.
BulkThe more we buy, the cheaperRice, atta, oil, mango boxes, dry fruits. Price tiers unlock as the group's total grows, and everyone pays the final tier.
ServiceBook together, pay less per flatPest control, tank cleaning, AC servicing, deep cleaning. The per-flat price drops as more flats join.
Market tripBook your share, buy togetherGo to the fish market or mandi together at a set time, or send one runner with everyone's list. Book your half now, pay at the market.

How a claim works

Ananya joins the basmati bucket with 10 kg. Her money is held, every neighbour's card updates live through Nexconn, and at the cutoff she pays the lower price the group unlocked.

Data model

What the server stores. A bucket can be created by any member, optionally with a vendor. Each share is either backed by a UPI hold or booked to pay at the market. When the bucket fills, an order is created and every share is copied into an order line with the member's details. Chat itself (messages, members, history) lives in Nexconn; the server keeps the money and the bucket rules, and links each bucket to its Nexconn card and channel.
