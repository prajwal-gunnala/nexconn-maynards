    Built on Nexconn

    Every conversation, card and call in the app runs on the Nexconn Chat and Call SDKs.

    Chat SDK

    • Chat UI kit for the street and bucket conversations
    • Group channels per street and per bucket; direct channels
    • Custom message cards: js:bucket js:event js:order js:otp
    • Live message metadata updates: cards fill in place as neighbours claim
    • Bot messages for pickup codes and order updates

    Call SDK

    • 1-to-1 calls between buyer and vendor
    • Group calls for the whole bucket before market day
